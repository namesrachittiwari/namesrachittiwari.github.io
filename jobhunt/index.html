Everything people ask before they let an AI loose on their career.

Will this actually get me hired?

Job Pilot finds, filters, pins, and preps. You still have to be likeable for 45 minutes on a video call — charm isn’t shippable yet. Everything before and after that call, it handles.

What’s a “ghost job”?

A posting that’s been “urgently hiring” since last Diwali. Job Pilot carbon-dates every listing and quietly buries the fossils so you stop writing cover letters to the void.

How is this different from LinkedIn alerts?

LinkedIn alerts are a firehose pointed at your face. Job Pilot is a filter with opinions — it reads the posting, checks the company, detects the red flags, and only then interrupts you.

Why is the reject button called “Yeet”?

Because “Archive” doesn’t capture the emotion of declining a role that wants 10 years of experience in a 6-year-old framework for an “entry-level” salary.
